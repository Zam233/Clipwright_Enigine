# 2018年

## What is the copyleft（2018年09月29日）
You may know something about copyright, and nowdays, the word &#34;Copyright&#​34; has become a well-known word.
From a positive prespective, copyright protects the right of creator, however, it also limited the freedom of other creators to create their works, and it becomes a tool for major company to making money and monopolization.
So is there any solution to solve this problem ?
And then, in June 1976, it is Wang Lichen(王理瑱) who first use the word &#34;Copyleft&#​34; in his BASIC for Intel 8080. Then, in 1985, the GNU Manifesto first explain the word &#34;Copyleft&#​34;.
GNU is not in the public domain. Everyone will be permitted to modify and redistribute GNU, but no distributor will be allowed to restrict its further redistribution. That is to say, proprietary modifications will not be allowed. I want to make sure that all versions of GNU remain free.
GNU不属于公共领域。GNU允许任何人修改和再发布，但是任何发布者都不能限制它的继续发布。就是说，它不允许专属性的修改。我想让GNU的所有版本都保持自由。
What is the relationship between copyleft and free software
In a word, Free Software is included in Copyleft, and copyleft is a set, and free software is the subset.
Why we should support copyleft and free software
In nowdays, most of user are resign themself to adversity, and be accustomed to proprietary programs. Free software give us another possibility and hope. Users, including all of you, have chances to totally control your devices, your systems, and no longer be afraid that the major company will sue you or do anything to you because all of the so called &#34;copyright&#​34;.
Karl Marx has explained the exploitation to the working class, and sweared to free the humanity from wage-labor. Free Software movement and copyleft will free the creativity, giving people the freedom to create their works and pursue their causes without any restrictions.
