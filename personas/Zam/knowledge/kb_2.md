## Where is your wage come from?（2019年03月07日）
After nearly two years, we will enter into every post of whole society, as a normal staff in company, I think you might be thinking where is your wage come from?
As you know, commodity is a labor product that can be exchange. If a thing can be a commodity, and it must have value and use value. First let we talk about the use value . If people wanna exchange this stuff, then that stuff must have use value or you will not exchange it for nothing. And use value is a attribute of a commodity but not only attribute of a commodity.
Since the different commodity can be exchange in the market, and they must have something that in same. At first we can deny that the same thing is not the use value. Such as rice can be ate, clothes can be wore, as you can see,eat and wear could not be same thing. So what is the same thing between the commodity? And that is celled the value .
Because of the labor also have double attribute, one is the Specific labor, the other is Abstract labor. As the Use value, the Specific labor could not be Quantify, but labor between labor can be compared, then remove the Specific labor, only have Abstract labor left. we can say that value is the abstract crystallization of labor. And These theory all above we called labor theory of value.
Then What can you get here? It shows that the value is the special phenomena in Commodity Economy, if there no market then no commodity. And then the Value is come from labor so that only means of production could not create value, but means of production as a commodity in market, it can move the value in means of production into the new commodity.
So you maybe ask, what the relationship between my wage and labor theory of value?
As you know, all kind of production are combination with worker and means of production, if your boss want organize the production, then he must pay the value of means of production and the value of labor, and it nearly same the value we produce the means of production before. But value of means of living which boss paid that can maintain your living are not equal with the new value you produced. That is to say that the value of original means of production are equal with the value of means of production in the commodity. But the value of original labor are not  equal with new value that create by labor.
So labor as kind of production factor, the value create by labor is a variable, and it can not equal with the value that boss gives you. And that difference between your wage value and new value that you created, we called Surplus value, or boss called it profit. The theory above that, we called surplus value theory.
So your wage as labor value ,it not depend on the value how much you created, also it not depend on the value what you created, instead like most of commodity,it depend on the value of necessary labor to create and maintain yourself
Someone will say  the marginal utility theory is also a economics theory, which just learn from economics math classes. Here is some criticize from me.
In Marxism Political Economics theory, Price of commodity decided by variable capital, Constant capital and surplus value. But in the marginal utility theory,  it say that labor is disutility, or the value of labor is decided by workers will, not material. So it decided that during the production, Workers and capitalist is in cooperation area, not in struggle area. Then the surplus value were covered.And contradiction of capitalism society are hidden.

下面，在正式讲资本主义的根本矛盾之前，在此不妨先粗略的讲一讲剩余价值论:
要讲剩余价值，就要先讲下生产资料。什么是生产资料。所谓生产资料，就是机器厂房，原材料，运输，道路桥梁等等。
剩余价值论构建在劳动价值论之上。简单来说就是，商品分为价值和使用价值。劳动分为具体劳动和抽象劳动。具体劳动创造使用价值，抽象劳动创造商品的价值。所以说，这也就是为什么说价值是劳动抽象的结晶。以上部分是劳动价值论的部分。
而一个商品的价值由生产资料的价值和劳动创造的价值两部分组成，在脱离劳动的情况下，生产资料是无法创造价值的，但是生产资料可以将其属于商品的价值转移到新的商品之上，这也就是说，商品中生产资料的价值和原来生产资料的价值是一样的。并且劳动也是参与生产的一环，但是劳动创造的价值是个不定量。言外之意，你创造的价值和你老板给予你工资中的价值是不对等的。你创造的价值减去老板给你工资中的价值就是老板们所谓利润的来源。就是这么简单。
怕各位听不懂，这里我简单举个例子，你生产了一件手机，价值1000元。1000元中耗费了800元的生产资料的价值，也就是包括机器，厂房原材料在内的。那么，也就是说，你生产这么一部手机，创造了200元的价值。但是你一个月要生产成千成万部手机，就拿最低的算，一千部就是二十万元，换而言之你劳动创造了二十万元的价值。但是你工资算上提成也只有不到6000元，那么二十万减去六千，有大约十九万跑到哪里去了呢?很明显就是流进了老板的腰包里面了。
