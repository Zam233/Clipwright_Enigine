摘  要

随着互联网的普及、新媒体技术的发展以及中国工业社会矛盾的出现，青年亚文化的规模在不断扩大，特别是B站的兴起更是一个标志性事件，而就在其中一类“哲学科普”类的亚文化在B站知识区异军突起，并逐步获得和扩大其之影响力。这一类亚文化诉说着理想，并且是“有理由”的理想。本文从“哲学科普”视频传播的内容和其媒介形象入手，旨在使用亚文化传播理论，从其选择的平台的属性开始，尝分析未明子在内容与形式上的亚文化因素，以及以此来分析支撑其内容得以发展的现实力量及其“否定性”，并尝试对其未来的发展走向进行一定的展望。

关键词：网络亚文化、内容传播、哲学科普


**ABSTRACT**

As the internet becomes more widespread, new media technology develops, and industrial society conflicts arise in China, youth subculture is constantly growing in scale. Bilibili's rise is a particularly notable event, and in it, one type of "philosophical popular science" subculture has emerged in Bilibili's knowledge section, gradually gaining and expanding its influence. This type of subculture speaks of ideals and is "reasoned" ideals. This paper focuses on the content of "philosophical popular science" video dissemination and its media image, using subcultural communication theory. Starting from the attributes of the platform it chooses, it attempts to analyze the subcultural factors in terms of content and form, and to analyze the real forces that support its content's development and its "negativity," and to make a certain outlook on its future development.

**Key Words:** Internet subculture, Content dissemination, Philosophical science popularization
